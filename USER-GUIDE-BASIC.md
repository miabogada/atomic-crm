# Clarklaw CRM — Staff User Guide

## Logging In

1. Go to **https://crm.tanoclark.com**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

After logging in you will see tabs at the top: **Accounts**, **Contracts**, **Tasks**, and **Contacts**.

---

## Viewing Your Tasks

1. Click the **Tasks** tab in the top navigation.
2. The list shows all open tasks, sorted by due date.
3. Use the **filter sidebar** on the left to narrow by account, assignee, or status.
   - Each filter can to be clicked to apply and clicked again to remove the filter. If you wonder why some items are "missing" be sure to remove all the filters.

To see tasks for a specific account:
1. Go to the **Accounts** tab and click the account name.
2. Click the **Tasks** tab inside the account.

---

## Opening a Task to See Its Details

Click on the **task text** (not the checkbox) to open the Task View panel.

The panel shows:
- Task description and notes
- Account and contract it is linked to
- Assignee and due date
- Status

From the Task View panel you can click **Edit** to make changes, or **Add Activity** to log a journal entry.

---

## Adding a Task

Tasks should be linked to a **Contract** whenever possible, not just an Account — this keeps the task history tied to the specific matter.

**Preferred — from the contract detail page:**
1. Open the account → **Contracts** tab → click the contract.
2. Click **Add Task**. The task is automatically linked to that contract.
3. Fill in the task form and click **Save**.

**From an account:**
1. Open the account → **Tasks** tab → click **Add Task**.
2. A contract picker appears — select the contract this task belongs to, or choose **Account level** if it does not relate to a specific contract.
3. Fill in the task form and click **Save**.

**Task fields:**
- **Task Text** (required) — What needs to be done
- **Task Type** — Email, Call, Meeting, Follow-up, Document Review, Filing, Court Date, Client Request, Document Request
- **Contract** — Link to the specific contract *(preferred over account-only)*
- **Assigned To** — Who is responsible
- **Due Date** — When it is due
- **Status** — To do, In Process, Blocked, or Done
- **Notes** — Additional details or a running journal of progress

---

## Updating a Task

**Option A — From the Task View panel:**
1. Click on the task text to open the Task View panel.
2. Click **Edit** at the bottom of the panel.
3. Make your changes and click **Save**.

**Option B — From the three-dot menu:**
1. Click the **⋯** (three-dot) menu at the right end of the task row.
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Make your changes and click **Save**.

**Fields you can update:**
- **Task Text** — What needs to be done
- **Task Type** — Email, Call, Meeting, Follow-up, Document Review, Filing, Court Date, Client Request, Document Request
- **Assigned To** — Who is responsible
- **Due Date** — When it is due
- **Status** — To do, In Process, Blocked, or Done
- **Notes** — Additional details or a running journal of progress

---

## Completing a Task

Click the **checkbox** to the left of any task to mark it done. This:
- Sets the status to "Done"

To undo, click the checkbox again.

## Adding an Activity (Journal Entry)

Activities record what happened — a phone call, email, meeting, or note. **Prefer linking to the contract** when the work relates to a specific contract, so the history is as specific as possible.

When you add an activity from a task that is already linked to a contract, the activity automatically inherits that contract link.

**From the Task View panel (recommended — links to the task and its contract):**
1. Click on the task text to open the Task View panel.
2. Click **Add Activity** at the bottom of the panel.
3. Fill in:
   - **Subject** (required) — A brief description (e.g., "Called client re: document status")
   - **Details** — Full notes about what happened
   - **Date** — Defaults to today (change if needed)
   - **Type** — Call, Email, Meeting, Document, Note, etc.
   - **Contract** — Pre-filled if the task is linked to a contract; otherwise select one if applicable.
4. Click **Save**.

**From the three-dot menu on a task row:**
1. Click the **⋯** menu on the task row.
2. Select **Add Activity** and fill in the form.

**From the Account Tasks tab (not tied to a specific task):**
1. Open the account → **Tasks** tab → click **Add Activity**.
2. A three-step picker appears:
   - Choose what to attach to: *A task*, *A contract*, or *Account level*.
   - Select the specific task or contract (if applicable).
   - Fill in the form and click **Save**.

The activity appears in the Tasks tab feed, nested beneath its parent task (if linked to one), or interleaved in the chronological feed (if account-level).

---

## Viewing Activities on an Account

1. Click the **Accounts** tab and open an account.
2. Click the **Tasks** tab inside the account. Tasks and activities appear together in a combined chronological feed.
   - Activities linked to a task are shown indented beneath that task.
   - Account-level activities appear interleaved with tasks in the feed.
3. Click on any activity to see its full details.

At the bottom of every account page there is also an **All Activity** timeline showing a chronological history of everything on the account — tasks, activities, notes, and more. If you don't see it, scroll to the bottom of the page and click on it to expand it.

---

## Viewing Payments on an Account

1. Click the **Accounts** tab and open an account.
2. The **Financial Summary** at the top shows at a glance:
3. For a full payment list, click the **Payments** tab inside the account.

To see payments tied to a specific contract and how they map to the payment schedule:
1. Open the account → **Contracts** tab → click the contract.
2. Scroll down to the **Payment Schedule** table to see each installment and whether it has been paid.
   - You may need to click the Payment Schedule to expand it if nothing is shown.

---

## Updating a Contact

1. Click the **Contacts** tab in the top navigation, or open the account and click the **Contacts** tab inside it.
2. Click the contact's name to open their record.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Update any fields.
5. Click **Save**.
   - Note: every Account should have at least 1 billing contact, which is normally the first contacct created. There's a billing contact toggle in each Contact item that can be set.

---

## Tips

- **Search**: Use the search box on any list page to find records quickly.
- **Sort**: Click any column header to sort by that field.
- **Filter**: Use the filter sidebar on list pages to narrow results by date, assignee, status, etc.
- **Mobile**: The CRM works on phones and tablets.
